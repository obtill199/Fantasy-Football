**Two structural facts:**

1. **You draft in pairs.** Back-to-back picks at every turn. Lets you take a fragile player and his handcuff in one breath, or double up ahead of a positional run.
2. **21-pick gap between 14 and 35.** Longest dead zone in the league. Whatever gets picked clean in rounds 2–3, you miss entirely. At pick 14 take the position that will be *barren* at 35 — not best player available.

---

## The Structural Read on 2026

**RB is expensive AND broken.** More RBs than WRs are now going in the first two rounds. For five straight seasons, fewer wideouts have finished inside the top 12 than RBs — in 2025 it was 7–4, with just 3 WRs inside the top 10. But August gutted the position: Jeanty, Jacobs, Breece Hall, TreVeyon Henderson, Jeremiyah Love, Kamara, Charbonnet, Dobbins, Monangai and Pacheco are all compromised.

**The market repriced the news; your league probably hasn't.** ESPN's default draft queue updates slowly and your leaguemates will draft off it. That is the edge tonight.

The play is not paying up for fragile RBs. It's owning the **healthy confirmed workloads** and the **direct backups** of everyone else.

## Execution Rules

1. **No full price for unknown Week 1 status.** Jeanty at 21 is a bad bet; Jeanty at 30+ is a good one. Same for Love (28) and Nabers (27) — let someone else eat those.
2. **If you draft a fragile starter, you must get his backup.** Jeanty → Washington. Kyren → Corum. Non-negotiable, and the backups are cheap.
3. **Target 6 RBs, weighted late.** The rounds 5–8 pocket is where workload certainty lives this year, not rounds 2–3.
4. **Wait on QB until round 9.** Herbert and Mahomes at ~104 means a top-8 QB at a bench price. Josh Allen at 33 costs you a starting WR.
5. **TE: top two or Pitts.** McBride (30) / Bowers (34), else skip to Pitts (81) or Kraft (103). Nothing between is worth a pick.
6. **K and DEF in the last two rounds only.** Aubrey goes at 129 in mocks — pure waste. Let them.
7. **Bye clustering.** Week 6 is brutal (Gibbs, Chase, St. Brown, Achane, Jefferson, Jameson Williams, Burrow). Week 11 close behind (Nacua, JSN, Bijan, London, Kyren, Watson). Don't stack three starters on either.
