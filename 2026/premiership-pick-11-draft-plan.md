## Philosophy (research, not projections)

- Pay early only when replacement at that position is ugly.
- 2026 RB supply is thin after the first two-plus rounds. Hero RB or Super-hero RB. Zero RB only if the board forces it.
- Wait on QB in 1QB.
- TE cliff: Bowers and McBride. After that, Loveland / Warren, then a drop.
- WR is the recovery position in Rounds 3–6.
- Late picks = contingent value (jobs that can change), not safe 8-touch veterans.

## Three scripts

### Script A — default winner: WR + RB

- `1.11` Jefferson or Lamb
- `2.03` Hampton / Chase Brown / Henry / Walker / Jeanty

At 35/38 take **WR + RB** or **WR + TE**.

### Script B — Super-hero if the room goes WR-crazy

- `1.11` Cook / Achane / Barkley
- `2.03` the other remaining featured back

Then smash WRs at 35 and 38 (Pickens, Olave, Nabers, DeVonta, Rice, McConkey).

### Script C — only if forced: WR + WR

Only if Jefferson **and** Lamb are both there, or Jefferson + London/AJ Brown and every featured RB is gone.

Then you **must** take an RB at 3.11. No TE, no QB, no upside WR3.

## Rounds 3–4 — picks 35 and 38

23 names come off while you wait. Expect gone:

- Last clean RB2s (Walker, Love, Kyren, Javonte start flying)
- Bowers and McBride
- Josh Allen, maybe Lamar

### If you have 1 RB

RB first at 35 — Breece Hall, Jeremiyah Love, Javonte Williams, Kyren only if cheaper than you have him, Travis Etienne. Then WR or Loveland/Warren at 38.

### If you have 2 RBs

Two of: Nico, Pickens, Olave, Nabers, DeVonta Smith, Rashee Rice, Tetairoa McMillan, Ladd McConkey, Zay Flowers. Or one of those plus Loveland/Warren.

### If you have 0 RBs

Take the best back at 35 and another at 38. Do not stay on a Zero-RB theory.

**QB can still wait.** Lamar at 35 is the one exception if the RB/WR tier is already dead. Even then, Hall / Love / DeVonta is usually better.

## In-draft rules

1. Track runs. Do not join them blindly.
2. Never take the 7th-best QB because two went.
3. Two-pick lookahead: if you take this WR, which RB survives 11 picks?
4. Stack only when the second piece is already BPA.
5. Bye weeks are Round 10+ hygiene, not a Round 3 veto.
6. Do not draft only off ESPN Best Available.

---

## One-sentence plan

Get one (preferably two) featured running backs before the position turns into committees, spend the middle of the draft on receivers the room is sleeping on, wait on quarterback, take a tight end only at a cliff, and use the last five rounds on injuries and jobs that can change in October.
