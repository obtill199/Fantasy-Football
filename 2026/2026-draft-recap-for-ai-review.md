## Preliminary self-assessment

### Strengths

- **RB foundation:** Taylor, Achane, and Pollard provide three playable workloads, while Spears directly hedges Pollard.
- **QB fit:** Maye's passing ceiling matters more in a six-point passing-TD league.
- **Bench asymmetry:** Hunter, Marks, Lemon, Coleman, and Spears can gain substantial value if their roles grow.
- **No early specialist premium:** Despite taking D/ST earlier than the master plan preferred, kicker was still delayed until Round 15.

### Main risks

- **Unproven WR room:** Egbuka, Flowers, and Burden have upside, but the roster lacks a traditional established target-dominant WR1.
- **Tight-end uncertainty:** Fannin may not immediately command a stable role, and using two roster spots at TE reduces flexibility.
- **Volatility and health:** Achane's workload/durability and the young receivers' weekly consistency could create a low floor.
- **Bye concentration:** Egbuka, Burden, and Lemon share Week 10; Maye, Fannin, and Juwan Johnson share Week 11; Taylor and Flowers share Week 13. This requires advance planning.
- **Specialists are replaceable:** Texans D/ST and Piñeiro should be streamed if matchup, role, or team environment changes.
